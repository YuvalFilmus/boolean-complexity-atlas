# Rooted-path AA statement: source and priority audit

23October2026. Fresh source check, separate from the earlier mathematical audit. No public edits. This audit supports **derived consequence of classical quadratic-chaos/Sidon methods**, with no verified publication of the exact native rooted-path formulation and no novelty certification.

## Findings that should affect presentation

1. The quadratic graph core already follows, in its order of growth, from published weighted Rademacher-chaos estimates. It must not be called a newly discovered analytic inequality. Our elementary oscillation proof supplies explicit constants and a transparent application.
2. The original sharp Khintchine statement is now directly checked: Szarek1976 Theorem1, printedp198. Replacing1/√3 by1/√2 gives A≤2√(2d)W, native maximum influence≥V²/(8dW²), and range[0,1] approximation using≤d ceil(2d/(δε²)) native queries. These are classical source-dependent constant improvements; preserve the self-contained12d version distinctly.
3. All tree paths, including incomparable endpoints, become quadratic under prefix characters. Published Boolean Bohnenblust–Hille already yields a polynomial-in-variance native influence consequence via the largest original coefficient. Thus the qualitative AA support-class assertion is not the strongest useful novelty framing. The rooted restriction improves the spectral-mass/variance-exponent estimate, while its exact application remains uncatalogued by this bounded search.
4. The Hadamard boundary example and random-sign bilinear method have close explicit original antecedents. They delimit an intermediate ℓ1/Sidon statement, not AA.

## Broader bound derived from the published coefficient theorem

Let K₂ be any valid dimension-free Boolean degree-two coefficient constant from Defant–Mastyło–Pérez Eq(2), and let p have nonconstant Fourier characters that become degree≤2 under an invertible character transform. After subtracting its expectation, its supremum norm is at most W, and coefficients/variance are preserved. Hence S=Σ|a|^(4/3)≤(K₂W)^(4/3). The largest native coefficient M satisfies V≤M^(2/3)S. Any nonempty native support contains a native coordinate, so

    max_native I≥M²≥V³/(K₂⁴W⁴).

This is a derived consequence, not a theorem quoted as being about rooted paths. For arbitrary tree paths of original length≤d, retaining m largest coefficients leaves squared error≤S^(3/2)/sqrt(m)≤(K₂W)²/sqrt(m), and querying their native supports costs≤dm. Thus for range[0,1] there is also a representation-available nonadaptive approximation using

    d ceil(K₂⁴/(δ²ε⁴))

queries. This differs from the rooted estimate d ceil(3d/(δε²)); neither uniformly dominates. It explains why the Hadamard example is not an obstacle to a broader polynomial AA consequence, even though it obstructs dimension-free ℓ1 mass.
